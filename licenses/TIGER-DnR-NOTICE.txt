tiger_music.onnx

Derived from the TIGER-DnR checkpoint by Mohan Xu, Kai Li, Guo Chen and
Xiaolin Hu (ICLR 2025), published at https://huggingface.co/JusperLee/TIGER-DnR
under the Apache License 2.0 (licenses/TIGER-DnR-LICENSE-APACHE.txt).

Changes made (Apache License 2.0, section 4(b)): the checkpoint was
converted to ONNX by charon-audio's tools/export/export_tiger.py. Only
the music network and its music output are kept; the STFT and inverse
STFT were moved out of the graph and are computed by the application.
The weight values were not changed. Source checkpoint SHA-256
dd1c696e72f6adea0085ef1af640882a8260519ad666422835e387a5b4abdd2a.

Training data, as stated in the TIGER paper: Divide and Remaster (DnR)
version 1, which mixes LibriSpeech speech, Free Music Archive music and
FSD50K sound effects. Many of those music tracks and some of the sound
effects are licensed by their authors for non-commercial use only.
